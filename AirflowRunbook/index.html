Privacy & Trust

Zero-knowledge operations.

Runbook is designed to connect directly to Apache Airflow installations controlled by its users. The developer does not operate a relay service and does not collect, track, or monetize personal information or Airflow instance data.

  • Keychain Storage

    Credentials are stored using the iOS Secure Keychain and never written to app group containers.

  • Direct HTTPS

    All network traffic goes directly from your device to your Airflow URL over TLS. No proxy or logging intermediary.

  • No Telemetry

    No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs. The demo workspace is fully synthetic and makes no network requests.
