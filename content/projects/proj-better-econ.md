BetterEcon puts configurable FRED® economic data widgets, values, and autoscaled sparklines on your iPhone and iPad Home Screen. Track unemployment, inflation, Treasury yields, mortgage rates, and any saved FRED® series without opening the app.

## What it does

- Choose any saved FRED® series for your Home Screen.
- Display the latest value or a dark-mode, autoscaled sparkline.
- Keep economic signals glanceable at a glance.

## Built with

- Swift, SwiftUI, and Swift Charts
- iOS 17+ Home Screen widgets
- Direct, device-side FRED® API requests
- BYOK API key stored in the iOS Secure Keychain
