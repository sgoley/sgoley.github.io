Linear Widgets Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

The developer does not operate a backend for Linear Widgets and does not collect, sell, track, or monetize personal information or Linear workspace data.

Credentials and requests

Your Linear personal API key is stored in the Apple Keychain. Live requests travel directly from your device to api.linear.app over HTTPS. Linear processes those requests under its own policies.

Local storage and widgets

Saved views, preferences, and cached issues are stored locally. The app and its widget extensions use shared local storage and a shared Keychain access group. Saved views and data are not synced through iCloud or between devices.

Demo, analytics, and tracking

Demo mode uses fictitious local data and does not access the network or Keychain. The app contains no third-party packages and no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Control and deletion

Disconnect in Settings removes the saved API key, saved views, and cached issues. Removing the app removes its application data subject to Apple platform behavior; use Disconnect first to remove credentials explicitly.

Support

When you contact support by email, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request. Do not send credentials or confidential workspace data. Contact Scottmgoley@gmail.com for support or privacy questions.
